# Reusable Job-Application Video Scripts

These are deliberately **not** one generic video to submit everywhere. Keep the middle mostly the same, but record a fresh take for each application and replace the bracketed sentence with the employer-specific line. A recruiter can tell when a video is reused without care.

All scripts land around 105-115 words including the company line — matches the ~120-word / ~50-second pace already field-tested in `job-application/Raya Holding/Video_Script.md`. Leave room for pauses. Use plain, natural English; do not try to sound like you memorised a corporate speech.

## 1. Software / Backend Version

**Use for:** .NET, backend, distributed systems, modernization, platform, fintech, integration, and full-stack roles where backend is the main work.

> Hi, I'm Moamen Basyoni, a software engineer from Alexandria.
>
> For around three years, I've worked on modernizing an enterprise healthcare platform using .NET, ASP.NET Core, SQL Server, APIs, and distributed services.
>
> The platform ran legacy .NET Framework, a single-database transaction workaround, and TFVC source control. I worked with the architect and development team to move it to Azure DevOps Git multi-repo with a private NuGet feed, Temporal Saga orchestration, an API gateway, and modern identity services.
>
> I enjoy this work because it's not just writing features — it's making a difficult system easier to evolve, deploy, and operate. [I am interested in **Company** because **company-specific reason**.] Thank you.

**Company-specific line examples**

- **Thndr:** “I am interested in Thndr because reliable money-movement systems need the same care around correctness, integrations, and failure handling that I enjoy working on.”
- **Müller’s Solutions:** “I am interested in Müller’s Solutions because this role is a strong match for my .NET, API, database, and modernization background.”
- **Geidea:** “I am interested in Geidea because payments is an area where reliability and secure integrations matter, and I have hands-on experience with payment webhooks and HMAC verification.”

## Recording checklist

- Record a **new take per employer**; only reuse the structure, achievements, and middle section.
- Use landscape, eye-level camera, clear front lighting, and a quiet room.
- Say the company name once, naturally. Do not over-praise the company or claim knowledge you do not have.
- Record 2-3 takes. Choose the relaxed one, not the most rehearsed one.
- If the portal allows one minute, aim for 45-55 seconds. If it gives another limit, budget roughly 120 words per minute of calm, non-native-English pace.
- **If the limit is under 45 seconds:** cut the middle paragraph down to its first sentence and drop straight to the company line — do not try to keep every item in the list. A shorter, unhurried delivery beats a rushed complete one.
- Do not claim hands-on Angular/React development, Kubernetes production operation, pgvector, AWS Bedrock, or seniority you do not have.
- When an application needs the video **now**, copy the relevant script into `job-application/<Company>/Video_Script.md` and adapt it like `Raya Holding/Video_Script.md` — with delivery notes, a setup checklist, and a shorter fallback if the limit is tight.

## Upload filenames

Use a clear filename per application:

`Moamen_Basyoni_Video_<Company>_<Role>.mp4`

Examples: `Moamen_Basyoni_Video_Raya_Holding_Software_Development_Specialist.mp4` and `Moamen_Basyoni_Video_Crossworkers_Senior_CSharp_DotNet_Developer.mp4`.
